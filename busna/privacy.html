This policy explains how the Busna app (“Busna”, “we”) handles your data when you use the app and its intercity bus seat-booking services. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

Busna is an intercity bus seat-booking app in Egypt. Privacy contact: Hossam Abdel Nasser, hn3232813@gmail.com, 01203670087.

Data we collect

  • Google account data: name, email address, and profile photo.
  • The phone number you add, and its verification status when SMS is enabled.
  • The governorate you select, and your university if you enter it (optional).
  • Booking data: route, boarding and alighting stops, date, seat, fare, and payment method.
  • Subscription data: plan, amount, payment status, collection date, and the inspector who collected it.
  • Support messages and feedback you send from the app.
  • Notification permission status and local app settings.

We do not collect precise GPS location; the governorate is chosen manually. We do not use your data for advertising or sell it.

How we use data

  • Create your account, sign you in, and manage your profile.
  • Complete bookings, issue QR tickets, and verify them at boarding.
  • Manage subscriptions, collect their price, activate them, and expire them.
  • Respond to support messages and resolve issues.
  • Protect the service and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Improve the quality of trips and the service overall.

Sharing

  • The driver or inspector sees your name, seat, route, boarding and alighting stops, fare, and ticket status to complete the trip.
  • Supabase: database hosting, authentication, and storage.
  • Google: Google sign-in.
  • An SMS provider when phone verification is enabled.
  • We may share data in response to a lawful request, or to protect our rights and users’ safety.

We do not sell your personal data or share it with advertisers.

Retention

We keep account data until you request deletion. Booking, subscription, and support records may be kept longer for operational, accounting, or legal purposes. Data is stored on Supabase servers and transferred over an encrypted connection.

Security

We use HTTPS, limited access permissions, and signed QR tokens. No system is 100% secure, so do not share your QR code or account data with others.

Your rights

  • Access your data and correct your name, phone, governorate, and university in the app.
  • Request deletion of your account and data by contacting support.
  • Withdraw consent by signing out and stopping use of the app.
  • File a complaint with the support team.

Children

The app is intended for university students aged 16 or older and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data.
